## My Favorite Series: Game of Thrones

**Genre:** Fantasy drama  
**Aired:** 2011 to 2019 (8 seasons)  
**Network:** HBO  
**Based on:** *A Song of Ice and Fire* by George R. R. Martin  
**Created by:** David Benioff and D. B. Weiss

### Why I Like It

*Game of Thrones* follows noble families fighting for the Iron Throne while a bigger threat gathers in the North. I like it because:

1. The story is full of twists, and no character is ever completely safe.
2. The characters are complex, and heroes and villains both have strong reasons for what they do.
3. The music and settings make the world feel real.

### Main Characters

| Character | Played by | Why I like them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tyrion Lannister | Peter Dinklage | Clever and funny, he survives by using his mind |
| Arya Stark | Maisie Williams | Brave and determined, she grows from a child into a fighter |
| Jon Snow | Kit Harington | Loyal and honorable, even when it is hard |
| Daenerys Targaryen | Emilia Clarke | Strong leader with dragons and big ambitions |
